    The road to the Platinum

    PowerPyx rates this Platinum 4/10 in difficulty and about 200 hours. It is long, not hard: one playthrough, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals, no online trophies. Follow the stages in order and the checklist does the rest.

      Before you start

      Cheats disable trophies, so leave them alone until the Platinum pops. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one more before the final mission.

      Missables & save discipline

      Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story.

      Your save plan

      1. Start of every chapter: make a manual save in a fresh slot. Never overwrite the chapter-start saves.
      2. Chapter 1, before it ends: do Lucia’s balcony meditation at Jason’s house (see Positive Mental Attitude).
      3. Before the final mission: make one more manual save, so you can return to free roam cleanly.
      4. After the story: keep one save labelled “post-story” as your cleanup base.

      There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so a manual save is the only way back. If a trophy ever fails to pop, the chapter-start save is your safety net.

      About this guide

      Grand Theft Auto VI released on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Its PS5 list holds 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze, 28 of them hidden until earned. Every trophy is single-player and offline. All guide text in this app is original writing for this entry; the trophy names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them.

      How the tracker works

      Tick a trophy and it is saved in your browser (localStorage) on this device. Export writes your progress to a JSON file; import reads one back. Nothing leaves your device and the app makes no network requests at all: it works with the network off.
